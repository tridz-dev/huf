# Remove Link at the Bottom of the Print Page

**Question:**

"How to remove the ERPNext link at the bottom of the page while printing it?"

**Solution:**

To eliminate the URL that appears at the bottom of printed pages, follow these steps:

1) Access the Print button.

2) Select "More Settings" from the available options.

3) Deactivate the "Headers and Footers" checkbox to hide footer content.

4) Click Save to apply your changes.
