# Sending Email from any Document

In ERPNext, you can send any document as email with a PDF attachment by clicking on Menu > Email after opening any document.

**Note:** You must have outgoing Email Accounts set up for this.

After clicking send, the email gets added to the email queue with a "Sending" status until it is sent. If sending fails, you can resend it by clicking "Send Now."

## Available Email Options

* **TO:** Recipients of the email
* **From:** Select which outgoing email account to use if you have access to multiple accounts
* **CC**: Carbon Copy to keep someone informed without directly addressing them
* **BCC**: Blind Carbon Copy—similar to CC but recipients cannot see who else received it
* **Email Template:** Use preset templates for standard replies; several are built into the system
* **Send me a copy:** Sends a copy to your email address to confirm successful delivery
* **Send Read Receipt:** "Ticking this checkbox will send you a notification if the receiver has read the email"
* **Attach Document Print:** Include the PDF of the document being sent
* **Select Attachments:** Add additional files

## Print Screen Fields

* **Select Print Format:** Choose the document's print format
* **Select Languages:** The language for PDF generation
