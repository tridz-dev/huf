# Printing and Branding

Documents sent to customers represent an organization's brand and image, requiring customization to meet specific needs. ERPNext provides multiple options for tailoring document branding to organizational requirements.

The platform supports printing barcode labels, POS receipts, and sending raw commands to printers through its Raw Printing feature.
