# Serial No and Batch Traceability Report

The Serial and Batch Traceability Report serves as a tool for monitoring complete movement and lifecycle stages of serialized or batched items throughout your supply chain. This system offers two distinct traceability approaches.

To access this report, navigate to: **Stock > Serial No and Batch > Serial No and Batch Traceability**

## Backward Traceability

This filter helps you identify which components were needed to manufacture a batch and determine their origins.

![Backward traceability visualization](/files/backward-tracebility.png)

## Forward Traceability

Use this filter to discover where a batch has been directed, which production stages utilized it, or which customers received it.

![Forward traceability visualization](/files/forward-tracebility.png)

## Backward and Forward (Both) Traceability

This option enables viewing the complete traceability chain in both directions.

![Combined traceability visualization](/files/both-tracebility.png)
