# Stock Transactions

Stock transactions in ERPNext encompass diverse activities for managing inventory movements across an organization. These include warehouse transfers, inventory adjustments, reconciliation processes, and recording of stock movements related to purchasing, sales, production, and consumption activities.

These operations are essential for maintaining precise stock levels, monitoring inventory flow, assessing performance metrics, and supporting strategic decisions regarding procurement, sales, and production planning.
