# Rules

Shipping and pricing rules are vital components within ERPNext's stock module that facilitate the oversight of shipping expenses and pricing methodologies.

## Shipping Rules

"Shipping rules allow businesses to define various parameters for calculating shipping charges, such as weight, volume, destination, or shipping method." These mechanisms automate cost computation during sales operations, guaranteeing consistent charges aligned with established benchmarks.

## Pricing Rules

"Pricing rules in ERPNext empower businesses to create dynamic pricing strategies based on specific conditions or criteria." Organizations can implement discount structures, promotional campaigns, tiered pricing for bulk orders, or customized rates tailored to distinct customer groups, product categories, or distribution channels.
