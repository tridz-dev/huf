# Pricing

In ERPNext's stock module, item pricing serves as a cornerstone for managing acquisition costs and sales values. The system enables businesses to assign distinct purchase prices to each item, reflecting true acquisition expenses.

ERPNext supports varied pricing configurations based on factors including:
- Currency specifications
- Customer group classifications
- Pricing rule parameters

This adaptability allows organizations to deploy multiple strategies—including volume discounts, promotional pricing, and customer-tiered approaches. Strategic item pricing facilitates accurate expense tracking, margin optimization, and customized pricing frameworks aligned with market conditions and customer preferences.
