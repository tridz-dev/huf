# Quick Stock Balance

**Quick Stock Balance is a tool that can be used to quickly retrieve the current stock balance (quantity) and value of an item, in the specified warehouse, on the specified date.**

It proves useful when conducting audits or verifying inventory particulars prior to making transactions.

To access this tool, navigate to:
> Home > Stock > Tools > Quick Stock Balance

## 1. Using Quick Stock Balance

The fundamental requirements are the Item and Warehouse fields to fully leverage Quick Stock Balance. You may alternatively use Item Barcode to retrieve stock information.

You have the option to choose a different warehouse or date. The system will fetch stock value and quantity in real-time based on your selections.

Additionally, you can access the Stock Balance report by clicking on 'Stock Balance Report'. This action will direct you to the report with Item and Warehouse filters already applied.
