# Serial and Batch

From version 16 onwards, enabling the serial and batch feature for items requires checking the 'Enable Serial and Batch No for Item' option in Stock Settings.

## Overview

ERPNext incorporates serial and batch tracking as fundamental inventory management capabilities. These features help organizations maintain unique identifiers for items throughout their lifecycle.

**Serial tracking** assigns distinct identifiers to individual units, supporting:
- Complete traceability from acquisition through distribution
- Warranty administration
- Item-specific identification during recalls or quality concerns

**Batch tracking** consolidates multiple items sharing common characteristics (such as production date or lot designation) into logical groups. This approach facilitates:
- Supply chain movement monitoring
- Regulatory requirement fulfillment
- Expiration date oversight
- Coordinated product recalls when needed

Both functionalities empower organizations to achieve "granular control over inventory management, enhancing traceability, quality control, and compliance efforts."
