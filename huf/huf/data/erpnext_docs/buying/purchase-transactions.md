# Purchase Transactions

Purchase transactions in ERPNext involve "the various activities involved in acquiring goods or services from suppliers."

The platform supports a complete procurement workflow that includes drafting purchase orders, receiving inventory, and handling invoices. ERPNext also enables supplier communication, maintains purchase history records, and connects with accounting systems for financial integration.

## Available Purchase Transaction Types

* Material Request
* Purchase Order
* Purchase Invoice
* Debit Note
* Request for Quotation
* Supplier Quotation
* Supplier Quotation Through Portal
* Purchase Return
