# Supplier Essentials

In ERPNext, supplier doctypes represent "the types of documents or records associated with managing suppliers within the system." By effectively leveraging these document types, organizations can enhance their procurement workflows, keep records accurate, and support timely purchasing operations.

The platform includes two primary supplier-related doctypes:

* [Supplier Group](/erpnext/user/manual/en/supplier-group)
* [Supplier](/erpnext/user/manual/en/supplier)
